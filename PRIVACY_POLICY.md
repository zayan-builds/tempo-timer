# Tempo — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 3, 2026_

Tempo ("the app", "we") is a speedcube timer for Android. This policy explains what
Tempo does with your information. In short: **Tempo collects no personal data.** All of
your solves, statistics, and settings live only on your own device.

## 1. Information we collect

Tempo does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you:

- **Solve history, statistics, and settings** are stored locally on your device using the
  device's own storage (Capacitor Preferences). They never leave your phone.
- **No account.** Tempo does not require or support accounts, logins, or email addresses.
- **No analytics, no ads, no crash reporting, no tracking SDKs.**
- **No location, contacts, photos, or microphone access.**

## 2. Permissions

- **Biometric lock (optional):** if you enable the app lock, Tempo uses your device's
  built-in biometric prompt (fingerprint / face) to authenticate you. Verification happens
  entirely on-device — no biometric data is read, stored, or transmitted by Tempo.
- **Storage (Android 7–9 only):** when you tap **Export**, Tempo writes your history file
  to your Downloads folder. On older Android versions this may require a one-time storage
  permission, requested only at the moment you export. Android 10+ uses scoped storage and
  never asks for this permission. Tempo never reads your files.

## 3. Updates

Tempo can check for updates through Capgo, a third-party update service. The update check
sends only your app version number to Capgo's servers. No personal data is shared. See
Capgo's privacy policy for their handling of that version metadata.

## 4. Data retention and deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you control it completely:

- **Delete your history** anytime from within the app (History → clear).
- **Uninstalling Tempo** permanently deletes all of its data from your device.
- Exported `.json` files in your Downloads folder are copies you created; delete them like
  any other file on your device.

## 5. Data sharing

Tempo does not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone. We have no access to
your data and cannot see your solves.

## 6. Children

Tempo is a general-audience utility and does not knowingly collect information from
children. Because no data is collected at all, there is nothing to restrict.

## 7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised and this page
updated.

## 8. Contact

For any privacy questions or concerns, open an issue on the project repository:
